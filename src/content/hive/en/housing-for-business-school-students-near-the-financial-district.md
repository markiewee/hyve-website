Housing for business school students near the financial district is a genuinely different search depending on which school you're at. If you're at SMU's Lee Kong Chian School of Business, your campus is already in the city. If you're at NUS Business School or NTU's Nanyang Business School, your actual campus sits nowhere near Raffles Place, and no room near your lecture halls changes that. What most business students end up wanting isn't housing near campus at all. It's housing that keeps an internship at a bank, a case competition, or a networking event downtown within easy reach, alongside a normal commute to class.

This guide covers where the three schools actually sit, why the CBD pull is real even for students whose campus is nowhere close to it, and how to think about the trade-off if you're choosing where to live.

## Where Are NUS Biz, NTU NBS, and SMU LKCSB Actually Located?

Only one of Singapore's three major business schools sits anywhere near the financial district. The other two are firmly on suburban campuses, a 30 to 45 minute MRT ride from Raffles Place on a good day.

**NUS Business School** is housed in the Mochtar Riady Building at 15 Kent Ridge Drive, on NUS's main Kent Ridge campus in the southwest of the island. Kent Ridge MRT (Circle Line) is the nearest station, with one-north and Buona Vista also within reach of the wider campus. None of them are close to the CBD. A direct Circle Line trip from Kent Ridge or one-north into the city still runs well over half an hour before you've added the walk on either end.

**NTU's Nanyang Business School** sits within NTU's main campus at 50 Nanyang Avenue, in Jurong West, on the far western side of Singapore. Students get in and out via a free shuttle bus from Pioneer or Boon Lay MRT station on the East-West Line ([NTU](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/contact-us)). It's about as far from the financial district as a Singapore campus gets, roughly 40 to 50 minutes door to door from Raffles Place on a direct line.

**SMU's Lee Kong Chian School of Business** is the outlier. It sits at 50 Stamford Road, part of SMU's compact City Campus in the Bras Basah/Bugis precinct, a short walk from Bras Basah and Bencoolen MRT stations and one or two stops from City Hall. This is genuinely inside or right at the edge of what URA defines as Singapore's Downtown Core ([URA](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/urban-design/downtown-core/)). SMU students aren't chasing CBD proximity. They're already standing in it.

## Myth: If Your Business School Isn't Near the Financial District, Living There Doesn't Matter

This is the assumption that trips up a lot of NUS and NTU business students when they're picking a room. The logic seems sound: your lectures, tutorials, and group project meetings all happen on campus, so why pay more to live somewhere else entirely.

It falls apart once you look at how a business degree runs in practice. Case competitions, banking and consulting info sessions, alumni nights, and internships are frequently held downtown, not on campus, because that's where the employers are. A room 40 minutes from Raffles Place is fine for term time and genuinely inconvenient for the parts of the degree that matter most for landing a job.

## Why Banking and Consulting Pull NUS and NTU Students Toward the CBD

Singapore's major banks and consulting firms cluster in the same stretch of the island: Raffles Place, Shenton Way, Robinson Road, Marina Bay. That's the Downtown Core by URA's own definition, and it's also where most banking and consulting internships, the ones business students spend their degree competing for, actually happen ([URA](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/urban-design/downtown-core/)).

For an NUS Biz or NTU NBS student, that creates a real split. Term-time life runs out of Kent Ridge or Jurong West. Internship season, case competition finals, and the recruiting events that lead to both run out of the CBD instead. A student who lands a summer analyst stint at a bank in Marina Bay isn't going to enjoy commuting in from the far west of the island for three months.

This is why a lot of NUS and NTU business students shift their housing search closer to the city once internship season or final year hits, even though their classes never move. It's about being near the office, the case comp venue, or the networking event that starts at 6:30pm and isn't worth a 45-minute trip home before it.

## SMU LKCSB Students Are Already Where Everyone Else Is Trying to Get To

If you're at SMU, this trade-off barely applies to you. Lee Kong Chian's campus already sits at the edge of the CBD, so an internship at a bank in Raffles Place or a case competition hosted downtown is a short ride, sometimes a walk, from your own lecture halls. SMU also runs almost no on-campus housing, which means SMU business students are choosing a neighbourhood from day one anyway, not adjusting later for internship season the way NUS and NTU students often do.

## Campus Housing vs CBD-Adjacent Housing: The Trade-Off

| | Living Near Campus | Living CBD-Adjacent |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Term-time convenience, cheaper rent, walking to lectures | Internship season, case comps, networking events, final-year recruiting |
| NUS Biz students | Clementi, Dover, one-north, short shuttle or MRT ride to Kent Ridge | 30-45 min from Kent Ridge, but short ride to Raffles Place once internships start |
| NTU NBS students | Boon Lay, Pioneer, Jurong East, on the free shuttle route | 40-50 min from campus, but positions you for internship season and city-based events |
| SMU LKCSB students | Largely moot, campus is already CBD-adjacent | Same answer either way, City Hall/Bugis/Bras Basah area IS the CBD-adjacent option |

Neither column is objectively right. A first or second-year NUS or NTU student with no internship yet usually gets more value staying close to campus, since most weeks are still lectures, not banking events. A penultimate or final-year student actively interviewing for banking or consulting roles has a stronger case for trading campus convenience for a shorter ride into the CBD.

## How to Decide Where to Live, Year by Year

- **First and second year, no internship yet:** prioritise campus proximity. Kent Ridge-adjacent areas for NUS, Boon Lay/Pioneer/Jurong East for NTU.
- **Internship search season:** weight your search toward whichever area shortens the trip to recruiting events and, eventually, the internship itself.
- **During the internship:** for a summer stint, a CBD-adjacent room for those months alone is often worth it.
- **SMU students at any year:** the campus location does most of this work for you already. Focus on rent and room fit instead.
- **Unsure which way an internship will go:** a location on a direct MRT line into the CBD keeps both options open. Lazybee's Ivory Heights sits on the North-South/East-West interchange at Jurong East, useful for NTU access and a direct line toward the city.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is NUS Business School near the CBD?**
No. NUS Business School is on the main Kent Ridge campus in southwest Singapore, roughly 30 to 45 minutes from Raffles Place by MRT.

**Is NTU's Nanyang Business School near the CBD?**
No. Nanyang Business School is part of NTU's main campus in Jurong West, typically 40 to 50 minutes from the financial district door to door.

**Is SMU's business school near the CBD?**
Yes. SMU's Lee Kong Chian School of Business sits at 50 Stamford Road, part of SMU's City Campus in Bras Basah/Bugis, at the edge of Singapore's Downtown Core.

**Why do NUS and NTU business students still want to live near the CBD if their campus isn't there?**
Banking and consulting internships, case competitions, and networking events that matter for landing a job are concentrated in the CBD, so students often shift their housing search closer to the city once internship season starts.

**Should a first-year business student prioritise CBD proximity over campus proximity?**
Usually not. Most of first and second year is lectures on campus, so proximity to Kent Ridge or NTU's Jurong West campus tends to matter more early on, with a shift toward CBD access once internship recruiting begins.
