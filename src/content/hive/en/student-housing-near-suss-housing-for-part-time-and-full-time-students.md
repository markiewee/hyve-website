## Student Housing Near SUSS: Where the Campus Actually Is

SUSS runs its main campus at 463 Clementi Road, Singapore 599494, in the Clementi/Bukit Timah corridor in the west-central part of the island. The nearest MRT stations are King Albert Park and Beauty World, both on the Downtown Line, plus Dover and Clementi on the East-West Line. Getting from any of those four stations to the actual campus generally means a short bus ride rather than a walk, using services like bus 74, 151, or 154 from King Albert Park's Exit A.

A newer Rochor campus has also been announced for SUSS, adding a second, more city-central location to the university's footprint over time. For now, Clementi remains the main site most students and staff are commuting to day to day.

## Two Very Different Kinds of SUSS Student

SUSS isn't a typical single-cohort university, and that shapes housing more than most students expect going in. It started life as UniSIM, built specifically to run part-time degree programmes for working adults, and only added full-time undergraduate intakes from 2014 onward, as part of a broader government push to expand local university places. Today it runs both tracks side by side: full-time undergraduate programmes for school-leavers and polytechnic graduates, and a large slate of part-time programmes aimed squarely at people already holding down a job.

That history matters because it means the housing question "where should I live near SUSS" doesn't have one answer. A full-time undergrad fresh out of poly or JC is optimising for proximity to campus and a social, student-heavy household. A working adult doing a part-time degree in the evenings is optimising for something else entirely: a stable base near both a job and a classroom, in a household of people on similar, working-adult schedules.

|---|---|---|
| Background | School-leavers and polytechnic graduates. Full-time intakes have run since 2014 | SUSS began life as UniSIM, built specifically for working adults, and still runs a large part-time slate |
| Optimising for | Proximity to campus and a social, student-heavy household | A stable base near both a job and a classroom, among people on similar working-adult schedules |
| Lease shape | Covering a semester or academic year cleanly, rather than running short and forcing a mid-term move | Multi-year and stable, working around an existing job |
| Commute test | Against a class schedule, factoring in the bus leg | From two anchors: the office and the classroom |
| Renting experience | Often the first time renting at all | Usually familiar, but with a different constraint |

SUSS does not operate an on-campus hall or PGP-style residence system the way NUS or NTU do, so every student sorts out off-campus housing from day one, not just the ones who miss a ballot.

## What Full-Time SUSS Students Should Look For

If you're a full-time undergraduate, a few things are worth prioritising when you're choosing where to live near the Clementi campus:

- **A workable commute to King Albert Park, Beauty World, Dover, or Clementi**, factoring in the bus leg from whichever station you're closest to, not just the MRT ride itself.
- **A lease that covers your semester or academic year cleanly**, rather than one that runs short and forces a mid-term move.
- **A furnished room with wifi and utilities bundled in**, since most full-time students aren't set up to buy and later dispose of furniture for a stay of a year or two.
- **A household that's actually liveable around exam periods and coursework**, not one that's silent on function and awkward on rent negotiation.

## A Few Common Questions

**What's the nearest MRT station to SUSS?**
King Albert Park and Beauty World, both on the Downtown Line, are the closest, along with Dover and Clementi on the East-West Line. All four generally need a short bus connection to reach the actual campus at 463 Clementi Road.

**Does SUSS have on-campus student housing?**
No. SUSS doesn't run an on-campus hall or PGP-style residence system the way NUS or NTU do, so students arrange their own housing off campus from the start, whether that's a private rental, staying with family, or co-living.

**Is SUSS only for working adults doing part-time degrees?**
No, not anymore. SUSS began as a part-time, working-adult-focused institution, but has run full-time undergraduate intakes since 2014 alongside its part-time programmes, so both types of student are on campus today.

**Should a full-time and a part-time SUSS student live in the same kind of place?**
Not necessarily. A full-time student is mostly optimising for proximity to campus and a student-friendly household, while a part-time student working full-time is optimising for a commute that works from both a job and a classroom, plus a longer, stable lease. See our [dedicated guide for working adults studying part-time](https://lazybee.sg/blog/mature-students-working-full-time-and-studying-part-time-housing-in-singapore) for that second case.

**Is co-living a good fit for a SUSS student?**
It tends to work well for both groups, since a furnished room with published lease terms removes the admin of landlord-hunting and furniture-buying that a first-time renter or a busy working adult usually doesn't have time for.
