No bee. Just the star and the dot.

Two shapes: A, the even seven-point star, and C, the same seven points weighted to a bee's proportions but with the body, head and bands stripped out. Each with the dot at three sizes, in two positions. Nestled sits the dot in the notch between the two upper points, so the star reads as a body and the dot as a head without ever drawing one.
