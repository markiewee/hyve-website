How it works

Four steps, and you can stop at any of them.

You tell us a month

Pick it above or send it on WhatsApp. We come back with the rooms that actually open then, including the ones not on this page yet because notice only just landed.

You see it

In person with whoever runs that house, or on a video walk-through if you are not in Singapore. Viewings need two working days of notice, because a real person has to open the door.

You hold it

One month deposit holds the room. No agent commission, because there is no agent. The tenancy agreement comes to you to read before any money moves.

You move in

Furnished, cleaned, wifi already working, keys and door code sent the day before. Bring a suitcase.
