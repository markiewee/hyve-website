Which language, where

The one rule that keeps four surfaces feeling like one company.

SurfaceGroundHeroHeadlinesPhotography
MarketingAlabasterFull-bleed photo, glass instrumentCormorantLeads the page
BookingAlabaster or TobaccoFull-bleed photo carouselGrotesk for placesLeads the page

Glass only over photography. One primary button per view. One accent, brass, and never a third thing in gold. Green appears as a band, never as a text colour on dark. Numbers are mono, always. Everything else is negotiable.
