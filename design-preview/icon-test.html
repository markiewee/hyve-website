Lazybee · the icon at real size

What actually survives 16 pixels.

Everything below is rendered to a real canvas at the real pixel size, then blown up with smoothing turned off, so you are looking at the actual pixels a browser would draw rather than a shrunk vector. That is the only honest way to judge this. The mark is the EVEN star, the symmetrical one.

Does the dot come with it?

In a square icon the lockup has to fit dot and star into the same box, so the star loses a third of its height. Drop the dot and the star gets the whole square. Compare the two rows.
