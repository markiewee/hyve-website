Lazybee · symmetry

How symmetrical do you want it?

Worth being exact about what is and is not symmetrical here, because it changes what to fix. The mark already has perfect mirror symmetry left to right: point 1 mirrors point 6, point 2 mirrors point 5, point 3 mirrors point 4, and the stinger sits dead on the axis. Fold it down the middle and the halves land on each other. What it does not have is radial evenness, because the seven points are different lengths. And no seven-point star can ever be symmetrical top to bottom, since seven is odd. One point always has to sit alone.

So the real dial is how much the point lengths vary. Below, from fully even to the bee weighting you have been looking at.
