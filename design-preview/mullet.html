The reference's body, your curves.

Your reference is a heraldic mullet of seven points: point straight up, straight edges, inner vertices at about 35 percent of the outer radius, which is where the mass comes from. Curvature turns out to be a single number in the same construction. At 1.00 the edges are dead straight and you get the reference exactly. Pull it down and the same shape grows concave sides, all the way to the needle star you have been looking at. Everything below keeps the mullet proportions and only moves that one dial.
