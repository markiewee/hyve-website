04 · Rules

The guidelines, short enough to obey

The lockup is vertical

Mark on top, wordmark under it, place name under that, all optically centred on one axis. A horizontal version exists only for the site header, and there the place name drops away.

Clear space equals the mark's height

Nothing enters that box. On the site that means the header gets more air than feels necessary, which is the point.

Never letterspace the body

The wide tracking belongs to the wordmark and to small caps labels only. Body copy set normally, or it reads as a perfume ad.

One accent, used sparingly

Gold, brass or honey appears on the CTA, the place name, and nowhere else. The moment a third thing turns gold, the whole page gets cheaper.

Photography is unstyled and warm

Real rooms, daylight, no wide-angle distortion, no empty-hotel-lobby staging. A crumpled linen sheet does more for this brand than a rendered vignette.

Copy is plain, never florid

The type carries the luxury. The words stay direct: what you get, what it costs, what happens next. No "curated", no "elevated", no "bespoke".
