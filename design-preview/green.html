Lazybee · placing the regal green

Give the green a job, not a slot.

The reason the green has nowhere to live is that we were treating it as a third palette competing with the other two. It works better as a structural colour that appears inside both. Three jobs it could hold, each shown in Alabaster and Tobacco so you are comparing like with like. The brass and the wordmark never change.

Option A

Green is the ink

Body and headline type go deep green instead of near-black. Grounds stay Alabaster and Tobacco. It is the quietest of the three and it changes the whole temperature of a page, the way a dark green ink changes a letterhead.

Option B

Green is the band

The page stays Alabaster or Tobacco, and one full-bleed green section carries the moment that matters: the proof numbers, the closing ask, the footer. Green becomes punctuation rather than atmosphere.

Option C

Green is the interface

Buttons, links, focus rings, active states, cards. Brass retreats to the wordmark and the place name only. Green does all the work where a person is being asked to act.
