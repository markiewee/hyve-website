# Everything public is open to everyone, including the AI crawlers. Being read by
# them is the point: they are the search surface we care about, and none of them
# are blocked below.
#
# Two closed doors. /portal/ is the authenticated tenant, captain, landlord and
# admin area. There is nothing behind it a crawler can reach anyway, and the
# pages it would see are served a noindex shell.
#
# /pitchv1/ is an investor deck that nothing on the site links to and that is not
# in the sitemap, so it was only ever reachable by someone who had the URL. It
# was nonetheless open to every crawler listed below, including the AI ones we
# deliberately invite. The link still resolves for anyone we sent it to; it just
# stops being something a search engine can hand to a stranger.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /portal/
Disallow: /pitchv1/
