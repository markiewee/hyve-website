Terms and conditions

1

Definitions and interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Common Areas" means the parts of the Property not let exclusively to any occupier, as described in Annex A, which the Tenant may use in common with the Landlord and other occupiers.

"Rent" means the monthly rent specified in Annex A.

"Room" means the room let to the Tenant with exclusive possession, as specified in Annex A.

"Security Deposit" means the deposit specified in Annex A, held by the Landlord as security for the Tenant's performance of this Agreement.

"Term" means the period from the Commencement Date to the Expiry Date, both specified in Annex A.

"Commencement Date" means the date specified in Annex A on which the tenancy begins, conditional on the Security Deposit and the first month's Rent having cleared.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. Words in the singular include the plural and vice versa. A reference to a Party includes that Party's successors and permitted assigns. References to this Agreement include its Annexes, which form an integral part of it, as amended in writing from time to time.

2

Rent and payment

2.1 The Tenant shall pay the Landlord Rent of S${{MONTHLY_RENT}} per calendar month, in advance and without deduction or set-off.

2.2 Rent is due on the payment dates set out in Annex D. Where the tenancy begins or ends part-way through a month, the Rent for that month is pro-rated on a daily basis.

2.3 Rent and all other sums are payable to the account specified above. The Tenant bears any charges imposed by the remitting bank.

2.4 If any sum remains unpaid for seven days after its due date, the Landlord may charge late payment interest at the rate stated in Annex A from the due date until payment is received in full.

2.5 Miscellaneous fees, including the air conditioning surcharge specified in Annex A, are billed monthly in arrears and payable within seven days of the Landlord's invoice.

3

Security deposit

3.1 On or before the Commencement Date the Tenant shall pay the Landlord a Security Deposit of S${{DEPOSIT_AMOUNT}}.

3.2 The Security Deposit is held as security for the due performance of the Tenant's obligations. It does not bear interest and shall not be treated as payment of Rent for any month, including the last month of the Term.

3.3 The Landlord may deduct from the Security Deposit any Rent or other sums outstanding, the cost of making good damage beyond fair wear and tear, the cost of replacing missing items, and any cleaning required beyond normal end-of-tenancy cleaning.

3.4 The balance shall be refunded to the Tenant within thirty days after the Tenant has vacated the Room and returned all keys and access devices, together with a written account of any deductions.

3.5 If the Tenant terminates the tenancy before the Expiry Date other than under Clause 10.3, the Security Deposit is forfeited to the Landlord without prejudice to the Landlord's other rights.

4

Stamp duty

4.1 Stamp duty on this Agreement is payable to the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore and is, by law, the responsibility of the Tenant.

4.2 As a matter of service, and at no additional charge, the Landlord will arrange and pay for the e-stamping of this Agreement on the Tenant's behalf and provide the Tenant with the stamp certificate. The Tenant need not hold a Singapore bank account to obtain a stamped copy.

4.3 The Tenant shall present the stamp certificate together with photographic identification to the estate management office when applying for access cards, and to any authority that requires evidence of residence.

4.4 Where the Tenant chooses to e-stamp the Agreement personally, the Tenant shall provide the Landlord with a copy of the stamp certificate within seven days of the Commencement Date.

5

Tenant's covenants

The Tenant agrees with the Landlord:

5.1 To pay the Rent and all other sums due under this Agreement on time.

5.2 To use the Room and the Common Areas solely as a private residence for the Tenant and any occupier named in Annex A, and for no other purpose.

5.3 To keep the Room in good and clean condition, fair wear and tear excepted, and to keep the Common Areas tidy and fit for shared use.

5.4 To bear the cost of repairing or replacing any item damaged by the Tenant or the Tenant's guests, and to bear the cost of minor repairs to the Room up to S$150 per item per occurrence. Repairs above that amount are the Landlord's responsibility under Clause 6.2 unless the damage was caused by the Tenant.

5.5 Not to assign, sub-let, part with possession of, or share the Room with any person not named in this Agreement.

5.6 Not to make any alteration or addition to the Room or the Property, and not to affix anything to walls, ceilings or doors in a way that leaves damage, without the Landlord's prior written consent.

5.7 Not to do anything that is a nuisance, annoyance or danger to the Landlord, the other occupiers or the neighbours, and not to cause the Landlord to be in breach of the by-laws of the estate.

5.8 Not to use the Room or the Property for any unlawful purpose, and to comply at all times with the laws of the Republic of Singapore, including immigration and work pass requirements.

5.9 To hold and maintain a valid pass permitting residence in Singapore for the whole of the Term, and to notify the Landlord immediately if that pass is cancelled, refused or not renewed.

5.10 To notify the Landlord promptly of any defect, damage, leak or pest issue affecting the Room or the Property.

5.11 To permit the Landlord access in accordance with Clause 9.

5.12 At the end of the Term, to yield up the Room and return all keys and access devices in the condition required by Clause 11.

6

Landlord's covenants

The Landlord agrees with the Tenant:

6.1 That the Tenant may quietly hold and enjoy the Room for the Term without interruption by the Landlord or any person lawfully claiming through the Landlord, subject to the Tenant paying the Rent and observing this Agreement.

6.2 To keep the structure, the roof, the main drains, the water and electrical installations, and the air conditioning system of the Property in proper repair and working order, except where the need for repair was caused by the Tenant or the Tenant's guests.

6.3 To pay the head rent, the property tax, and the maintenance or service charges payable to the estate management, so that nothing done or omitted by the Landlord puts the Tenant's occupation at risk.

6.4 To attend to reported defects within a reasonable time, and to attend as a matter of urgency to any defect that makes the Room unsafe or uninhabitable.

6.5 To arrange servicing of the air conditioning serving the Room at reasonable intervals.

6.6 To furnish the Room and the Common Areas as described in Annex B, and to keep those items in working order except where damaged by the Tenant.

7

Occupancy and guests

7.1 Only the Tenant and any occupier named in Annex A may reside in the Room.

7.2 The Tenant may host guests. A guest may stay overnight for no more than three consecutive nights, and no more than seven nights in any calendar month, without the Landlord's prior written consent.

7.3 The Tenant is responsible for the conduct of the Tenant's guests as if it were the Tenant's own, including any damage they cause and any breach of the House Rules at Annex C.

7.4 The Tenant shall not advertise, list or offer the Room or any part of the Property for short-term accommodation on any platform.

8

Utilities and services

8.1 The Rent includes electricity, water, gas, wireless internet, and the cleaning of the Common Areas at the frequency stated in Annex A.

8.2 The air conditioning surcharge in Annex A applies to usage above the stated daily allowance and is billed separately under Clause 2.5.

8.3 The Landlord is not liable for any interruption to a utility or service caused by the supplier, the estate management, or any cause outside the Landlord's reasonable control, but shall take reasonable steps to have the supply restored.

8.4 The Tenant shall not use the Room in a way that places an unreasonable load on the electrical installation, and shall not operate any high-consumption appliance not supplied by the Landlord without prior written consent.

9

Access and inspection

9.1 The Tenant has exclusive possession of the Room. The Landlord and any person authorised by the Landlord may enter the Room only:

9.1.1 with at least twenty-four hours' written notice, at a reasonable hour, to inspect the state of repair, to carry out repairs, or to show the Room to a prospective tenant during the last two months of the Term; or

9.1.2 without notice, in an emergency involving a risk to life, health or the fabric of the Property, such as fire, flood, gas escape, or a report of a serious incident.

9.2 The Landlord retains a key to the Room for the purposes set out in Clause 9.1 and shall not use it for any other purpose.

9.3 The Tenant shall not change the lock to the Room, or add any lock, without the Landlord's prior written consent, and shall provide the Landlord with a key to any lock so consented to.

10

Term, renewal and termination

10.1 The tenancy runs for the Term specified in Annex A and expires on the Expiry Date without the need for notice.

10.2 If the Tenant wishes to renew, the Tenant shall give the Landlord written notice at least one month before the Expiry Date. Renewal is at the Landlord's discretion and on the terms then offered.

10.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement before the Expiry Date by giving one month's written notice, provided the tenancy has run for at least three months on the date the notice takes effect. Rent remains payable to the end of the notice period.

10.4 The Landlord may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if:

10.4.1 any Rent remains unpaid for fourteen days after its due date, whether formally demanded or not;

10.4.2 the Tenant is in material breach of this Agreement and has failed to remedy that breach within seven days of written notice requiring it;

10.4.3 the Tenant no longer holds a valid pass permitting residence in Singapore; or

10.4.4 the Tenant uses the Room or the Property for an unlawful purpose.

10.5 Termination does not affect any right or remedy already accrued to either Party.

10.6 Where the Tenant vacates before the Expiry Date other than under Clause 10.3, the Tenant remains liable for the Rent until the earlier of the Expiry Date or the date a replacement tenant takes occupation, together with the Landlord's reasonable costs of re-letting.

11

Expiry and handover

11.1 On or before the Expiry Date the Tenant shall remove all personal belongings, return the Room and the items listed in Annex B in the same condition as at the start of the Term, fair wear and tear excepted, and return all keys and access devices.

11.2 The Room shall be handed back clean. If professional cleaning is required beyond normal end-of-tenancy cleaning, the cost is deductible from the Security Deposit.

11.3 Any belongings left in the Room or the Common Areas more than seven days after the Tenant has vacated may be disposed of by the Landlord, and the cost of disposal is deductible from the Security Deposit.

11.4 If the Tenant remains in occupation after the Expiry Date without the Landlord's written agreement, the Tenant shall pay double the daily Rent for each day of holding over, without creating a new tenancy.

12

Estate management and house rules

12.1 The Tenant acknowledges and agrees to comply with the House Rules at Annex C, and with the by-laws and regulations of the estate management of the Property.

12.2 Access cards, keys and other access devices remain the property of the Landlord or the estate management. The Tenant shall pay the replacement cost of any that are lost, damaged or not returned.

12.3 The Tenant shall register with the estate management office where required, and shall provide the documents that office reasonably requires, including the stamp certificate under Clause 4.3.

12.4 The Landlord may amend the House Rules on reasonable written notice where required by the estate management or by law, or where necessary for the safety or quiet enjoyment of the occupiers. No amendment may increase the Rent or shorten the Term.

13

Indemnity

13.1 The Tenant shall indemnify the Landlord against all claims, damages, costs and expenses arising from any breach of this Agreement by the Tenant, or from any act or omission of the Tenant or the Tenant's guests, except to the extent caused by the Landlord's own negligence or breach.

13.2 This indemnity survives the expiry or termination of this Agreement.

14

Insurance

14.1 The Landlord insures the structure and the Landlord's own contents. That insurance does not cover the Tenant's personal belongings.

14.2 The Tenant is responsible for insuring the Tenant's own belongings and is encouraged to hold personal contents and liability insurance for the Term.

14.3 The Tenant shall not do anything that invalidates the Landlord's insurance or increases the premium payable, and shall reimburse the Landlord for any increase caused by the Tenant.

15

Limitation of liability

15.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes either Party's liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for any liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

15.2 Subject to Clause 15.1, the Landlord is not liable for the loss of or damage to the Tenant's belongings, however caused, unless caused by the Landlord's negligence or breach of this Agreement.

15.3 Subject to Clause 15.1, the Landlord's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed a sum equal to two months' Rent.

16

Force majeure

16.1 Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations, other than an obligation to pay money, caused by an event beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, epidemic, act of government, or failure of a public utility.

16.2 If such an event renders the Room uninhabitable, the Rent shall abate from the date the Room becomes uninhabitable until it is fit for occupation again. If the Room remains uninhabitable for more than thirty days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice, and the Landlord shall refund any Rent paid in advance for the period after termination.

17

Notices

17.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing.

17.2 Notices from the Landlord to the Tenant shall be sent to the email address specified in Annex A, and are deemed received on the day of sending unless a delivery failure is received.

17.3 Notices from the Tenant to the Landlord shall be sent to hello@lazybee.sg, and are deemed received on the next working day.

18

Waiver

18.1 No failure or delay by either Party in exercising a right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of that right, and no single or partial exercise prevents any further exercise of it.

18.2 The acceptance of Rent by the Landlord after a breach is not a waiver of that breach.

19

Assignment

19.1 The Tenant shall not assign, transfer or otherwise deal with this Agreement or any right under it.

19.2 The Landlord may assign or transfer this Agreement on written notice to the Tenant, provided the Tenant's rights under it are not diminished.

20

Severability

20.1 If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be severed and the remainder shall continue in full force and effect.

21

Entire agreement

21.1 This Agreement, including its Annexes, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties in relation to the Room and supersedes all prior discussions, representations and arrangements.

21.2 No variation of this Agreement is effective unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.

22

Governing law and dispute resolution

22.1 This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Singapore. The Parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Singapore courts.

22.2 Before commencing proceedings, the Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute by discussion, and may refer the dispute to mediation at the Singapore Mediation Centre.

22.3 The Tenant and the Tenant's guests shall not use the Room or the Property in any manner that infringes the laws of the Republic of Singapore, including its rules, regulations and by-laws.

Annex C

House rules

Community standards and conduct requirements. The Tenant acknowledges and agrees to the following.

  1. Keys, key cards and other items used to gain physical access to the Room or the Property remain the property of the Landlord or the estate management. The Tenant shall safeguard them and shall be liable for replacement fees should any be lost, stolen or destroyed.
  2. The Tenant shall promptly notify the Landlord should any key, key card or access device be lost, stolen or destroyed.
  3. Quiet hours are observed from 10.00pm to 8.00am. Noise shall be kept at a level that does not disturb the other occupiers or the neighbours at any time.
  4. Smoking, including electronic cigarettes and vaporisers, is not permitted anywhere within the Property.
  5. Cats are permitted with the Landlord's prior written consent. No other pet may be kept without prior written consent. The Tenant is responsible for any damage, cleaning or pest treatment arising from a pet.
  6. The kitchen, laundry and living areas are shared. The Tenant shall clean up after use, shall not leave personal items in the common areas, and shall remove personal food items that have expired.
  7. Refuse shall be bagged and disposed of in the designated chute or bin. Recyclables shall be separated where the estate requires it.
  8. Parties and gatherings that exceed the guest limits in Clause 7.2 are not permitted without the Landlord's prior written consent.
  9. Candles, incense, portable gas stoves and other open flames are not permitted anywhere within the Property.
  10. Illegal drugs and unlicensed firearms are strictly prohibited. Any breach is a material breach entitling the Landlord to terminate immediately under Clause 10.4.
  11. The Tenant shall comply with the by-laws and regulations of the estate management, including rules on facility booking, visitor parking, and moving in or out.
  12. The Tenant shall not conduct any business, trade or commercial filming from the Property.
